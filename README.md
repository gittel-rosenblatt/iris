This will be my app for the Congressional App Challenge. My app is called Iris, after the part of the eye responsible for controlling the amount of light that enters the eye. 

My father has Retinitis Pigmentosa, which is a genetic eye disease that causes loss of vision over time. There is no cure and no real cause. I was inspired to make Iris because I know he struggles immensely with filling out forms online. Iris is a dashboard that can help people with impaired vision fill out their forms without struggling.

I hope to one day be able to do more.
