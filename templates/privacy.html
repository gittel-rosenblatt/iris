PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Welcome to Iris ("we," "us," or "our"). We built Iris to make digital PDF forms accessible to everyone, including visually impaired users. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how it stays safe.

1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

Personal and Profile Information

We collect information that you voluntarily provide when you create an account or set up your profile. This includes:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Usernames and passwords
  • Birthdays
  • Physical addresses

Note on v1/v2 Architecture: Some profile fields (like address and birthday) support our automated form-filling features. They are saved in your account profile so you can reuse them, and they remain stored until you update or delete them.

Uploaded Files (PDFs)

When you upload a PDF to make it accessible, we store that file in our database so you can access, read, and manage it across sessions. Your uploaded files stay stored until you explicitly delete them from your dashboard or close your account.

Sensitive Information

We do not intentionally collect or process special categories of sensitive personal data. Because our tool helps read and fill general PDFs, please avoid uploading documents containing highly sensitive medical or financial identifiers unless necessary for your workflow.

2. HOW DO WE PROCESS YOUR INFORMATION?

We process your data strictly to deliver and improve Iris:

  • Account & Authentication: Managing your login and securing your profile.
  • Service Delivery: Rendering and storing your uploaded PDFs and profile data.
  • AI Processing: Sending text structure from uploaded PDFs through Google Cloud AI to perform document parsing, layout extraction, and text analysis. Google Cloud AI processes this data under enterprise API terms and does not use user-submitted documents to train public models.
  • Support & Communication: Responding to bug reports or account inquiries sent to contact@irisforms.org.

3. WHEN AND WITH WHOM DO WE SHARE YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION?

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or uploaded documents to third parties.

We only share data in these limited scenarios:

  • AI Subprocessor: Google Cloud AI (as described above) strictly to parse document text.
  • Business Transfers: If Iris is ever acquired or merged, user data would transfer under the same privacy commitments.
  • Legal Compliance: If required by valid legal process or to prevent imminent harm.

4. COOKIES AND TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES

We use basic session cookies and local storage strictly to keep you logged in, remember your accessibility preferences (like high-contrast or text size), and prevent site crashes. We do not use cross-site advertising trackers.

5. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE-BASED FEATURES

Our parsing tools rely on Google Cloud AI. Uploaded content is transmitted via encrypted API requests solely to convert inaccessible PDF layouts into screen-reader-friendly text.

6. DATA RETENTION

Your profile data and uploaded PDFs remain stored persistently in our database for as long as your account is active or until you delete specific files. If you delete an item or your account, the data is removed from our active systems.

7. DATA SECURITY

We use HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit and standard cloud database security controls at rest. No internet transmission is 100% immune to risk, so we encourage strong, unique passwords.

8. MINORS AND USERS OF ALL AGES

Iris is open to students, creators, and individuals of all ages, including participants in events like the Congressional App Challenge. We do not impose an artificial 18+ ban.

9. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS & DATA CONTROL

You can view, edit, or delete your profile information and uploaded PDFs at any time directly in your account dashboard. To request a complete account wipe, email us.

10. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy as Iris evolves. Significant changes will be noted with an updated date at the top.

11. CONTACT US

Questions? Reach out anytime at:
