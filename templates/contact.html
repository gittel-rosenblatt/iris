Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to some common questions.

What is Iris?

Iris is an accessible form assistant designed to make filling out digital forms stress-free and straightforward for the visually impaired.

What is Iris's mission?

Iris is dedicated to lighting up the digital world by breaking down barriers to online accessibility. We believe that navigating the internet should be equitable, stress-free, and seamless for everyone. By transforming complex digital paperwork into clean, accessible user experiences, Iris empowers visually impaired individuals to navigate essential online spaces with absolute independence and confidence.

Is Iris free to use?

Yes! We want to keep digital spaces open and accessible to everyone who needs them.
