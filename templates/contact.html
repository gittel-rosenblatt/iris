Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to some common questions.

What is Iris?

Iris is an accessible form assistant designed to make filling out digital PDF forms stress-free and straightforward for the visually impaired.

Why was Iris created?

After watching my father struggle with his paperwork, I realized digital PDF forms are a huge accessibility barrier for people with visual impairments. The goal of Iris is to break down this barrier and more like it one step at a time.

Who is Iris for?

Iris was designed with visually impaired people in mind, but anyone can use it!

Is Iris free to use?

Yes! Iris will stay 100% free as long as it remains running. We want to keep digital spaces open and accessible to everyone who needs them.

How does Iris work?

Iris works in five simple steps:

  1. Make an account and log in.
  2. Navigate to your dashboard, click "Choose PDF File," and upload the PDF of your choice. Note: Iris works best with interactive PDFs.
  3. Go question-by-question through your document and answer how you'd like.
  4. Review your answers in a read-only preview.
  5. Click "Generate PDF" and download your PDF! Now you're done!
How can I get involved?

We'd love to hear your thoughts on how to improve Iris! Feel free to send us and email using the form or DM us on Instagram.
