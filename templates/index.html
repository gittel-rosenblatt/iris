About Iris

This is Iris, named after the part of the eye responsible for controlling the amount of light entering the eye. Our goal is simple: to make filling out online forms less of a headache for the visually impaired.

When you create an account with Iris, you enter basic information about yourself, such as your full name and email. Then, when you upload a form to fill out, Iris automatically fills in the appropriate blanks with your information. Then Iris goes through with you and helps you fill out the remaining blanks. One more once-over with text-to-speech, and your form is ready for download.

Iris is a simple, easy-to-use tool to make your life a little easier. You can try Iris by clicking the “Get Started” button on the right. I can’t wait to see how Iris helps your digital confidence bloom.

If you have any questions or suggestions, my contact information is in the footer. Don’t hesitate to send me an email, DM me on Instagram, or leave a message.

About Iris's Founder

Hiiiiii, my name is Gittel Rosenblatt, and I am the founder of Iris. Iris was (is?) my project for the Congressional App Challenge in Florida.

I’ve been learning to code on and off for around a year. This project taught me a lot about web design, Python (specifically Flask), JavaScript, and how to take a project from an idea to reality. This past summer, I participated in the Girls Who Code Pathways program, and I think it’s safe to say their web development coursework helped me immensely in bringing Iris to life.
