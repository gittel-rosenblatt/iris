About Iris

This is Iris, named after the part of the eye that controls how much light enters the eye. Our goal is simple: to make filling out online forms less of a headache for the visually impaired.

Iris transforms static form filling into an accessible, guided workflow. Simply upload your document, interact with clearly organized form fields, and listen back to your input with built-in text-to-speech before saving your completed file.

Iris is a simple, easy-to-use tool to make your life a little easier. You can try Iris by clicking the “Get Started” button on the right. I can’t wait to see how Iris helps your digital confidence bloom!

If you have any questions or suggestions, contact us through the contact form or email us at contact@irisforms.org. Don’t hesitate to reach out; we’d love to hear from you!

What's Next for Iris

We’re constantly improving Iris to make form filling even more effortless. Here is a preview of what’s coming next:

  • Form Autofill: Securely saved user profiles that automatically pre-fill recurring fields—like name, address, and contact details—upon upload.
  • Document Renaming: Flexible PDF customization, allowing you to rename your files directly within the app after uploading.
  • Visual Accessibility Themes: Personalized display settings that let users customize font styles, text sizes, and color contrast modes on their dashboard.
  • Flat PDF Parsing: Improved handling of flat PDFs for better compatibility.
  • Other Accessibility: Other accessibility features based on user feedback.

We’re always looking for ways to improve Iris. If you have any suggestions, please don’t hesitate to reach out!
